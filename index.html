02 — Spotlight project
AeroSense
Smart India Hackathon 2026Team Nostromo · 609/26 — Now

A handheld, ML-integrated breath analyser that screens exhaled breath for drug consumption using an electrochemical sensor pad, aiming for an on-the-spot result in under a minute.

◎ Touch Mode

Coined from our police survey: if a suspect refuses to blow or gives too little breath, a fingertip sweat sample on the same pad is analysed instead.

% Machine learning

Trained a scikit-learn classifier on 40 breath sessions with session-grouped validation: 91% cross-validated accuracy.

⚡ Backend + app

Built a FastAPI backend and Flutter companion app that receive live ESP32 readings and store results and test history.

♥ Clinically informed

The team consulted a pulmonologist on breath-sampling conditions to shape how the device collects samples.
